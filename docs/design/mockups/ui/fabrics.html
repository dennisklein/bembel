A full member talks to all members. A limited member talks only to full members. In kestrel-lustre the limited clients cannot reach each other on 0x0002; they still share Default and kestrel-ipoib. The default partition 0x7fff is not editable.

Rails of osprey-roce

rail = one leaf, one VLAN, one /24
Port k of every GPU node goes to leaf k. NCCL and RoCEv2 traffic of a rail stays in its VLAN. The links come from FNT Command. The VLAN and the subnet come from bembel.
